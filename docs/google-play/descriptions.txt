App name (30):
Mangaka AI – Manga Maker

Short description (80):
Write, draw and letter your own manga. Script, panels, canvas, bubbles, export.

Full description (4000):
Mangaka AI is a complete manga studio for your phone. Start from a one-line idea, write the script, lay out the panels, draw with real inking tools, place the speech bubbles and export a finished chapter – all in one app, all saved on your device.

WRITE YOUR STORY
• Outline your story in acts and chapters on a drag-and-drop board.
• Write scene by scene with setting, action, dialogue, narration and sound-effect blocks.
• Type @ to tag a character in a line of dialogue.
• Find and replace, word counts, page estimates, long-dialogue warnings.
• Keep character profiles (role, traits, goal, voice, appearance) and a world wiki for places, factions, terms and events.

LAY OUT YOUR PAGES
• Auto-paginate a chapter: the script becomes pages and panels in one tap.
• 25+ panel templates: grids, diagonal cuts, bleeds and 4-koma strips.
• Drag panel borders, cut panels with a line, merge, tilt corners, set gutters.
• Right-to-left manga pages (B5 or A5) or vertical webtoon strips.

DRAW ON YOUR PHONE
• G-pen, pencil, brush, eraser, shapes, lasso fill and a colour picker.
• Screentone fills at 10/25/40/60% density.
• Up to 8 layers per panel with opacity and lock, 50 undo steps.
• Two-finger zoom and rotate, two-finger tap to undo, stylus pressure support.
• Pin a character design sheet on the canvas as a reference.

LETTER AND POLISH
• Speech, thought, shout, whisper and machine bubbles, narration boxes and SFX text.
• Auto-place bubbles from the script, then drag, resize and rotate them.
• Speed lines, focus lines, tones, gradients and sparkles.
• Add language versions of every bubble for translated editions.

READ AND SHARE
• Read your chapter like a real manga with page spreads in landscape.
• Export to PDF, PNG pages (ZIP), CBZ or a long webtoon image.
• Back up a whole story as a single .mangaka file and import it on another device.

AI WRITING HELP (optional)
Connect Mangaka AI to your own OpenAI-compatible server and get help with the words: shape a story from an idea, get three plot directions, break a chapter into scenes, continue or rewrite a scene, fix a character's voice, shorten a line, suggest sound effects, fill a character profile from a few keywords, find plot holes, translate bubbles and chat with an assistant that knows your story. Every AI result is a preview you accept or discard. Without a server the whole app works as a manual manga studio.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your stories, drawings and characters stay on your device. No account is needed. Nothing is uploaded unless you set up an AI server yourself.

Made for anyone who wants to make comics: first-time creators, webtoon artists, doujinshi circles and students of manga.
