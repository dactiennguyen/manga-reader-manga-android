Everything a mangaka needs

Six stages, one app. No subscription, no account, no upload.

  • 01

    Write the story

    Outline acts and chapters on a drag-and-drop board, write scene by scene, tag characters with @, keep profiles and a world wiki.

  • 02

    Lay out pages

    Auto-paginate the script, pick from 25+ templates, cut panels with a line, merge, tilt corners, set gutters. Manga or webtoon.

  • 03

    Draw on your phone

    G-pen, pencil, brush, lasso fill and screentones at four densities. Eight layers per panel, 50 undo steps, stylus pressure.

  • 04

    Letter and polish

    Speech, thought, shout and whisper bubbles, narration boxes, SFX text, speed lines, focus lines and tones.

  • 05

    Read and share

    Read like a real manga with spreads in landscape. Export PDF, PNG pages, CBZ or one long webtoon image.

  • 06

    Optional AI help

    Plug in your own OpenAI-compatible server for plot directions, rewrites, character voices, translations and a story-aware assistant.
